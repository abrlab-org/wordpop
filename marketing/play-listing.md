**Full description** (4000 char max)
```
WordPop! turns learning English into a puzzle.

A picture appears — a cat, a rocket, a rainbow — and you tap the scrambled
letter tiles to spell its English name. That's the whole game. No menus to
learn, no rules to read.

BUILT FOR BEGINNERS
Most word games assume you already speak English. WordPop! doesn't. It starts
with simple three-letter words and grows with you, one letter at a time, so
you are never thrown in at the deep end.

454 WORDS ACROSS 15 THEMES
Animals, food, home, nature, people, places, transport, sports, school,
clothes, body, colours, feelings, music and everyday things — the concrete,
useful vocabulary you actually need first.

HEAR EVERY WORD
Tap "Say it" to hear any word pronounced out loud. Reading a word and hearing
it are different skills, and WordPop! teaches both.

NOTHING TO LOSE
There are no timers, no lives, and no game over. Get a word wrong and the
tiles simply come back so you can try again. Stuck? A hint reveals the next
letter.

WORKS ANYWHERE
Tiny download, and it plays fully offline — no connection needed once
installed. Perfect for the commute, the classroom, or anywhere data is
expensive.

NO ACCOUNTS, NO TRACKING
No sign-up, no personal data collected, nothing sent to our servers. Your
progress stays on your device.

Whether you are learning your first English words or you just enjoy a
relaxing word puzzle, WordPop! is a few quiet minutes well spent.
```
