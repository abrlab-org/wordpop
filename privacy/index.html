WordPop! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

WordPop! is an English vocabulary game published by ABR Lab. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. WordPop! has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no servers of our own. We never see who you are or how you play.

What stays on your device

Your progress — score, streak, hints remaining, and how many words you have cleared — is saved locally on your device so the game can resume where you left off. It never leaves the device, and it is not linked to any identity. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

Advertising

The app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, which funds free distribution. Ads are the only third-party component in WordPop!. Google may process a device advertising identifier in order to serve and measure those ads. That processing is governed by Google's own policy, not ours:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device.

Children

WordPop! is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and because the app collects no personal information at all, none is gathered from any user regardless of age.
